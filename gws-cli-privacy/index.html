gws-cli Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What gws-cli is

gws-cli is a private, personal automation tool. It is not a public product or service. It is used only by its developer, Jan-David Soutar, to work with his own Google accounts from his own computer.

What it accesses

When the account owner approves it, gws-cli can read and manage data in that same person's own Google account: Gmail (read, search, and create drafts), Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Slides. It does this only when the account owner runs it or asks his own assistant software to.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the account owner's own computer, to help him manage his own mail, calendar, tasks, and documents.
  • Sign-in credentials are stored encrypted on that computer. They are not sent to any other server.
  • gws-cli does not send email on anyone's behalf and does not publish anything. Drafts are reviewed and sent by the account owner.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell Google user data.
  • We do not share Google user data with third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, or to train generalized artificial intelligence models.
  • We do not allow people to read the data, except where the account owner reads his own data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deleting access

Data is kept only on the account owner's computer, under his control, and he can delete it at any time. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops gws-cli from reaching the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
